## Goal and priority

The concise operating contract is [docs/PROJECT_PRIORITIES.md](docs/PROJECT_PRIORITIES.md): **(1) fully standalone original APLs per supported role; (2) truthful, efficient native/headless validation; (3) verified cross-spec resolution transfer; (4) human/ChatGPT leadership with Codex ONLY on explicitly delegated bounded tasks; (5) immutable rollback/approval protection and a friendly openly AI-developed hobby ethos.** GitHub fun facts are separate from actual APL correctness and may not drive artificial commits.


Build ONE self-contained, adaptive, safe, castable Hekili Forever APL per supported spec/role. Joint first priorities: **Druid Guardian/Feral Tank (catalogue Druid_Guardian) AND Hunter Beast Mastery (catalogue Hunter_BeastMastery), equal priority → Druid Feral DPS → remaining roles**, unless a critical cross-spec safety or current-release compatibility regression pre-empts them. Maintain levels 1–60 and viable ST/AoE, levelling/world/dungeon/raid scenarios without inventing data.

**Portfolio-first experiment rotation:** while every Supported role does not yet have a meaningful pinned latest-release *combat/final-fallback* native assessment, target approximately **3/4 new role-specific verified units** on under-served specs outside Guardian/BM/Feral, with cross-spec issue classification and independent transfer tests. These are flexible *units of real evidence*, not an hourly PR/commit requirement; unfinished PRs and urgent safety faults take precedence. Source import-only passes do **not** count as validated rotation coverage. See [ROLE_COVERAGE](docs/knowledge/ROLE_COVERAGE.md) and [project priorities](docs/PROJECT_PRIORITIES.md). Never rush unsafe changes to meet a coverage quota.

**Project-wide APL autonomy requirement (20 Nov 2026):** every supported role's complete APL must run its **normal rotation without the player having to enable a per-spec setting/tick box**. Put upkeep, maintenance, pet/form, resource, threat and safe cooldown decisions in the APL itself using verified source observables. Generic Hekili toggles are **optional user off-switches**, never an excuse to hide necessary fillers or routine tank upkeep; explicitly honour deliberate OFF choices and the engine's native Require Toggle restrictions (a pure APL cannot override them). See [APL Autonomy Standard](docs/APL_AUTONOMY_STANDARD.md) and the all-revision [control audit](scripts/audit_apl_controls.py). Protected old settings-dependent rollbacks remain untouched until a separate source- and in-game-validated Candidate displaces them.

**Continuous headless development and weekly gameplay hand-off (21 Nov 2026):** Gameplay-only evidence is an **approval/promotion gate for the affected Candidate**, **not a stop-work gate for the entire project**. When a Guardian, Hunter BM or other role cannot progress meaningfully without the player's real client, document its exact complete Candidate path/SHA, known safe/unknown result, current/last-working rollback, focused in-game next-three/final-fallback test and `/hek snap` acceptance in [the current live checklist](docs/Tests_P0_LIVE_ACCEPTANCE_V011.md) (or its current-release successor). Leave it unselected at **Pending In-Game Validation** (or Draft with an explicit pending test), then move the **next AI Sprint** to a distinct, worthwhile source-verified alternative: another equal-P0 role, Feral DPS, other supported role, new full standalone Candidate architecture, or source-native harness/Belief regression. Preserve negative and positive headless evidence. Do **not** repeatedly stop because the *same* live-client task is pending, invent cast/damage data, promote/replace a working file, or generate empty CI/commits to simulate activity. Human player testing is organised into a **Saturday morning UK gameplay checklist**, prioritised and small enough to play; avoid demanding immediate play during hourly development. Continue bounded autonomous engineering under the existing short-lived PR, exact-head CI, integrity and protected-main rules. If no genuinely useful, source-testable task remains in one bounded run, report the specific constraint and choose a new direction next run; never treat that as a blanket freeze.

Shipped WoWSims-derived/default rotations are research references and test comparators **not architectural ceilings**. Consider original priorities, talent/level guards, pet/form/stance/cooldown/defensive/utility options and safe end-of-chain fallbacks, only when the current released engine actually supports the required actions and observations. Respect player Major/Minor Cooldowns, Defensives, Interrupts and Potions toggles; verify named items and helper states.

## Universal efficiency rule: Research once → Preserve → Reuse

This rule applies to **all work**, not just recurring APL bugs: spell/talent discovery, user toggles, engine/API/Belief constraints, fixture design, regression triage, CI architecture, security, docs and release review. **Before new work, query existing verified knowledge and previous failed attempts; after new evidence, record the minimum reusable source-pinned fact, scope, outcome, uncertainty and next decision; before reusing, validate applicability.** Preserve positive AND negative evidence and exact hashes/rollback. Never repeat research or build a new abstraction solely to appear productive. Avoid churn, bulk sweeps, gratuitous AI/Codex calls or duplicated documentation.


**Cross-Spec Research Multiplier (owner instruction, 28 November 2026):** Every material positive or negative mechanic/engine/Belief finding triggers a risk-proportionate applicability scan across all 23 supported roles. Record exact reviewed baseline/action family, source pin, originating evidence, receiving-role applicability (Applicable / Not Applicable / Needs Evidence / Blocked), reason, next independent test and outcome in [CROSS_APL_TRANSFER](docs/knowledge/lessons/CROSS_APL_TRANSFER.md) or its linked evidence. Search current/development baselines first, then affected candidate branches; reuse unchanged prior scans. Confirm receiving-class mechanics and observability before transferring a guard or claiming a pass. DoT research distinguishes accepted press, actual landed aura, expiry/refresh, tick cadence, snapshotting, target identity, resources/points, channel interaction and uncertainty. Apply the same process to every other mechanic family. A scan is not a measured APL improvement, and must not create bulk speculative edits or block unrelated progress. Prioritise actionable defects and verified gains. All imports remain standalone.

## Main-first development and branch lifecycle

**Owner lifecycle decision (28 November 2026; supersedes earlier folder conventions):** Each Supported role has exactly one protected development rollback in `roles/<Class>/Current/` (v1.0 by default; Feral DPS v1.6). `Candidates/` contains only active full standalone Draft alternatives above that protected revision. `Archive/` contains all older/superseded revisions, rejected or abandoned candidates and former rollback files, for historical research or exact recovery only. On explicit owner approval/designation of a replacement, relocate the replacement to Current, archive the former Current and other superseded candidates, and continue with the next numbered Draft. Do not change a gameplay approval or last-working designation merely because a file moves. Preserve original APL bytes, SHA-256, revision identities, negative evidence and exact last-working references. Historical test comparators may resolve through Archive by exact hash. Do not create new Checkpoints copies; reconcile existing ones by exact hash without losing distinct evidence. Migration must be an atomic, separately validated PR updating catalogue paths, rollback registry, active fixtures/scripts/workflows, path-keyed allowlists, docs and links; preserve immutable run evidence and verify all hashes before and after. Never move files blindly while conflicting PRs are open. No physical migration or gameplay promotion is implied by this policy-only change.

**Canonical working location is protected `main`**, not a growing set of permanent feature branches. Keep verified source/knowledge/docs and active standalone Draft Candidates on main in `roles/<Class>/Candidates/`. Current, Candidates and Archive follow the owner lifecycle decision above; until the atomic migration lands, historical paths remain authoritative. Always check catalogue status, `preferred_apl`, `last_working_apl` and hashes. **`latest_revision` does not select a Candidate.** Merge-to-main or green CI does **not** promote the Candidate.

For each bounded change: start from the latest `main`, create **one short-lived PR branch**, preserve exact old files, run cheap validator plus relevant pinned source-native tests, merge to protected `main` when reliable, and **delete the merged branch**. Branch cleanup is **automated by the narrowly scoped trusted-main [cleanup workflow](.github/workflows/safe-merged-branch-cleanup.yml)**, with exact same-repo merged-head SHA and open-PR/protection checks. It runs on main pushes with a weekly fallback, uses GitHub's short-lived token (no PAT), and never checks out an untrusted PR head. Never delete unmerged/unreviewed/changed branches or bypass main protection, and do not accumulate permanent development branches. Read the [one-page navigation and workflow](docs/WORKING_FROM_MAIN.md) before creating another branch.

## Our upstream boundary and latest-release responsibility

**Hekili Forever is an independent addon, developed and released by `zmsl/hekili`; APLForever does not control its source, roadmap, releases, API or defect fixes.** Our responsibility is to maintain **our own** complete, optimised standalone APLs against the **latest actually published Hekili Forever release** and the real engine/Belief/API support it provides. Do not wait for control of upstream, assume an upstream default is superior, patch or pretend to maintain the addon, or claim that a source-native test can force an upstream fix.

**Current release pin and compatibility status:** [machine-readable release record](docs/knowledge/engine/current-release.json) and [dated release ledger](docs/knowledge/engine/RELEASE_LEDGER.md). **Automated detection, NOT APL updates:** [daily published-release watcher](.github/workflows/monitor-forever-release.yml) opens exactly one review issue when GitHub announces a newer stable tag. No PR/branch/APL changes, blanket revalidation, automatic version bump, status promotion or in-game proof result from that watcher. On a release: verify tag+SHA, source changes, warning/condition/kit support and shipped comparator; run fast then **risk-scoped** source-native validation for impacted roles, include Final Fallback/Repeated Recommendation, retain immutable Current+last-working, publish only separately validated Draft Candidates. Flag incompatible roles/engine limitations honestly; do not falsely mark whole-project compatibility green.

**Never conflate three statuses:** latest *published* upstream version; latest *source-tested* version in a bounded scope; and an individual APL's **in-game proven** state. Source patch availability or a future unpublished commit is not a released guarantee. Under a new release, review Guardian Tank and Hunter BM equally first, then Feral DPS and risk-ranked remainder; genuine broad engine change justifies a whole-role gate. Source-native failures from inherited APLs are tracked and cannot be silenced to claim success.
