## Why this distinct current-release test matters

[Earlier v0.10.0 five-case full-v1.5 source proof](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44759624040) established a client pseudo-role **DAMAGER** even when the Guardian pack runs, and observed `aggro` safety versus the rejected `group`-only unsafe alternative. The later [v0.11 Bear-level test](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44824089560) verified low-level accepted casts but **did not independently test** v0.11.0 client role + `UnitThreatSituation` true/false under real Rage. This test is that exact missing release compatibility checkpoint; do not reclassify historical v0.10 results as current. Shipped source/default remains comparator, not architectural constraint.

## Exact released-source experiment

[Native source CI 44891425933](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44891425933): **5/5 tests PASS**, zero failure/error. Each test imports/shares three complete, separately executable profiles: **full shipped default**, immutable v1.0, full unselected v1.5. Same seed **30417**, 4-yard enemy, published Guardian level-60 Bear kit, two source-natural enemy attackers, source-earned Rage, no injected resource, and `SCENARIO="game"` (NOT the fake simulator-only `maintain_demoralizing_roar=true` checkbox). Native Major/Defensives/Movement OFF. The test overrides **only** the *source fake client* pseudo-role and plain `UnitThreatSituation("player")` return to cover each scenario; this threat input is **synthetic**, not proven on a player's screen. Genuine source-world attacks, Rage gains, 15 non-vacuous simulation profile executions, and successful/refused cast events determine outcomes. The actual shipped/draft packages' normal full fallback chains run to the end.

Every case: **zero refused casts** for shipped, working and candidate, at least three actual source-accepted actions and three hostile swing events, zero candidate Barkskin with native Defensives OFF. In the two positive aggro cases v1.5 used Roar **once**, not repeatedly, with three subsequent source-accepted GCD/ability actions and no immediately duplicated Roar. Source-observed maximum `rage.lo` was **44.75** in positive candidate cases; this is naturally earned belief, not a crafted reserve. `trueFinalRage=2.75` in those 38s tests is not an efficiency, threat or survivability advantage. No simulated DPS ranking can be inferred from a greater accepted count (17 vs 13/18). Output observations and next-three checks cover bounded tested chains, not a proof that every possible no-action end is reachable or safe.

**Important default comparator nuance:** the shipped source-world's real-game-mode optional maintenance setting is off by default, so shipped did **not** cast Roar despite aggro. Working v1.0 explicitly gates on `settings.maintain_demoralizing_roar`, also off. v1.5's actual source-accepted Roar is **self-contained** in this state, not dependent on that per-spec tick box; user-configured native Ability Require Toggle is independently authoritative and was not overridden.
