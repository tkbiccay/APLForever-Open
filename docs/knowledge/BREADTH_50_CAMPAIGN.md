## Counting rules

One unit is one NEW independently supported, role-specific, falsifiable safety/rotation/coverage hypothesis with published Forever source pin, actual native executed positive/negative comparator evidence, source-versus-APL-versus-harness diagnosis, and next decision. A discovered genuine failure with precise diagnosis is evidence even when a safe APL replacement cannot yet be justified. Do **not** count seed reruns, compilation/import-only coverage, CI reruns, docs-only changes, PRs without an actual new finding, unmerged pending tests, or ambiguous gain claims. Prefer an item only once source-native CI passes, evidence/limits are logged and protected PR merged. Preserve previous full APL versions and all rollback hashes. The word 'approved' in this campaign grants AUTONOMOUS DEVELOPMENT permission, not an `Approved` APL status. No APL promotion without in-game proof.
