## Cross-spec learning (reusable verified patterns)

1. **After an upkeep or proc, check downstream source actual effects:** FoF generation + charge consumption + unique Ice Lance hit, real combo point spend *or legal retention on avoidance*, real Maul queue+strike, actual three channel ticks, true DoT ticks. Avoid comparing only accepted icons.
2. **Final Fallback / Repeated Recommendation is universal:** every fully reachable tail including low level, no-resource and repeated presses. A legal repeated Frostbolt/Searing Pain can be a damage/DoT-uptime issue, not a refused-cast bug. A source terminal rejected Garrote remains a logged unresolved edge.
3. **Short solo vs sustained vs extreme resource:** test 15–30s solo, 90–135s stationary and true 5% resource when supported; reserve the user's immersion-first gold/mana/positioning/level logic over unsupported raid-only theory. Do not spend 250 Mana on a totem for 0–1 hits and declare success.
4. **Never fix an engine/API limitation by inventing an APL expression:** health/triage, pet readiness, hidden Mana/Rage, live melee dead zone, unsupported forms and weapon imbue observability must be honestly logged. Avoid user settings checkbox dependencies, honour native off-switches.
5. **Source first, safe in-game proof last:** protected main/CI and hashes are necessary but not enough to displace an actually reported-working APL. On regression roll back; no guaranteed DPS proof without real measured damage and survivability.
