## Safe import, same method for all roles

Record installed Forever addon version, character level, learned rank/talents, gear (for shield, stance/pet tests), existing active pack, native toggle states and game context. Preserve the prior pack **exactly**. Type `/hekili` → **Priorities**, enter a **new Priority Name**, choose the exact specialisation, **Create New Pack**; open its **Source** tab, paste the **entire APL** into *Priority (SimulationCraft Action Priority List)*, click **Import**, inspect all warnings, then set **Active** on this test pack only. Abort on unknown action/expression or disabled essential line. Restore the prior pack afterwards. Source: [official v0.11 custom priority tutorial](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/docs/wiki/APL-Tutorial-Your-First-Custom-Priority.md). Do **not** enable custom per-spec checkboxes to make regular upkeep work; respect deliberately disabled native cooldown/defensive off-switches.

Use safe, level-appropriate **open-world enemies**, not non-existent Forever target dummies. Capture **first icon → actual accepted cast → next three actual accepted actions**. Repeated identical recommendations are not automatically failures if a real legal cast occurred or an intentional wait applies; repeated *ready but unusable/no progress* icons are regressions. Never assume hidden HP, pet health, target count, aggro or range is observable.

## Equal P0: Guardian Tank v1.6

It should recommend and successfully cast Roar at most when useful, then continue with **three actual legal attacks**, not a stuck repeat. In a short ~9-second solo fight it should not spend the costly Roar prematurely. Use a separate safe test for native Defensives ON/OFF: Barkskin respects the user off-switch while ordinary Roar upkeep should not demand an extra settings checkbox. Do not disable an important defensive in a dangerous pull. If client `aggro` or debuff/range cannot be read, record **Engine Limitation**, not a fabricated success or unjustified APL failure. The new **v1.6** adds a strict source-supported `gcd.remains=0` Barkskin guard after v1.5 emitted **three world-GCD-refused icons** in a matched native test. This is not proof of real client GCD timing or damage mitigation. Test actual Barkskin buff/cooldown, whether a defensives-ON cast occurs under realistic threat, and whether the next three actions remain legal; note that source v1.6 also used Barkskin with simulated no-client-aggro but two genuine attackers, so do not automatically call that a wasted defensive. Preserve v1.5 as a failed-source-timing comparator; **do not use it instead of the latest test-only v1.6**. See [v1.6 limits](Limitations_Druid_Guardian_v1.6.md). ## Equal P0: Hunter BM v1.4

With **Lone Wolf intentionally selected** (where the actual talent is present), neither Call Pet nor Revive Pet should be recommended, even if the source client reports an absent/dead pet. With Lone Wolf **not** selected, verify Call/Revive only on naturally absent/dead pet states, including the next three legal actions after a successful pet arrival. The v1.3 full Draft is the protected experimental comparator; v1.4 changes only the Lone Wolf Revive guard in precombat and combat. Do not infer pet health or live arrival from source-native icon repetition.

With a naturally absent living pet and Call Pet learned/usable, observe **recommended Call Pet → actual successful arrival → recovery icon disappears → three later legal casts**. If another safe pull occurs naturally, verify upkeep and no stuck permanent-pet **Call Pet** after arrival. The separate temporary **Summon Hawk** can legitimately recur as its 18-second guardians age (cap two, shared 6s Arcane Shot cooldown); the source-only [three-pull test 44991615130](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44991615130) accepted 3–4 temporary Hawk casts per fight but **cannot model actual in-game guardian arrival or damage**. Test uncastable first-icon repetition, not raw summon count. If a pet **naturally dies**, repeat with Revive Pet only when safe; **do not kill it just to test**. The addon knows pet presence/death, not pet HP, so keep Mend Pet manual. At **level 25+** (source rank unlocked), in naturally safe **6–8-yard** spacing, Summon Hawk can be legal while Aimed/Arcane/Serpent or Rapid Fire may be out of range; expect no repeatedly *uncastable* ranged shot. With native Major Cooldowns deliberately **ON**, talented Bestial Wrath may be cast in close proximity when the real pet is alive; it must respect its long cooldown. **OFF** must suppress it. No requirement to wait two minutes solely for testing. [v0.11 six-yard experiment](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44809978166) measured two legal 145s modelled Wrath casts t=3/123, **not actual game uptime**. ## Capture and rollback

For any wrong icon, use `/hek snap` (or `/hekili snapshot`) **while the problem is shown**; then `/hekili` → **Snapshots (Troubleshooting)** → latest → Ctrl+A/Ctrl+C complete snapshot, plus warnings and errors. Official [v0.11 reporting instructions](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/docs/wiki/Reporting-A-Problem.md). Record **role, game/addon build, level, talents, gear, APL revision/hash, toggles, target, expected icon, actual icon, whether actually cast, next three actual accepted actions, repeated behaviour, and full snapshot**. Restore old active pack immediately on any meaningful regression. Result labels: **Pass / Fail & Rollback / Inconclusive / Not Applicable (unlearned) / Engine Limitation**. Only after genuine practical evidence, fair default/working comparisons and no critical issues can a role progress towards In-Game Validated or Approved.
