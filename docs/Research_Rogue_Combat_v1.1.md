# Research — Rogue Combat v1.1 (Forever)

**23 November 2026.** Support **Supported** by catalogue. Target: original standalone safe level-60 Rogue Combat opener priority; no engine patch. Pin published **Hekili Forever v0.11.0** source `zmsl/hekili@86b739cbc4e6afbcbc693c3790ee81197fc6234b`; source mechanics: `src/Hekili_Rogue/Rogue.lua` and `tests/Forever/helpers/rogue_world.lua`. The shipped WoWSims-derived priority is the comparator, **not the design ceiling**. Full protected Current v1.0 source and own fallback retained as rollback.

**Hypothesis:** low starting Energy delays Garrote until after the game's/source-world pull clears Stealth, but the priority's `buff.stealth.up` branch continues to recommend it, starving the final Sinister Strike fallback. Preserve genuine Vanish and Ghostly Strike override branches while requiring ordinary Stealth opener to be out of combat.

**Confirmed in source-native diagnostics:** two seeds at 25 Energy, Current `garrote` attempted every 0.2s and failed `requires:not stealthed` 501 times, zero accepted combat actions. Source-world actual Energy was 90–100 when failed (not an Energy shortage at refusal time). Shipped advanced 55/58 accepted, 0 refused. This is source-truth/Belief divergence not sufficient to claim real-client bug without `/hek snap`. Exact in-memory one-expression guard yielded 64/63 accepted and no source refused, normal-Energy counts unchanged [native 45093516898](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/45093516898).

**Production candidate design:** fully standalone v1.1 changes only `actions.opener=garrote` guard from `(buff.stealth.up|buff.vanish.up|buff.ghostly_strike_next.up)` to `((!combat&buff.stealth.up)|buff.vanish.up|buff.ghostly_strike_next.up)`. No new action, ID, variable, setting, default dependency or user checkbox. Verified source language expression `combat` and `buff.*.up` in original APLs. The rest of the 1–60 kit and all fallbacks untouched.

**Fair comparison:** [stored APL 4/4 PASS 45093860815](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/45093860815) validates exact byte-match to one-line hypothesis and complete shipped, protected Current and Candidate source imports at 100/25 Energy, two seeds; cooldowns OFF, natural 10 Energy/s world regeneration, melee range, 75/100 seconds. New v1.1 is unselected `Draft`, **not Approved or performance-better**. Opposing factors to test: solo ST Current intentionally withholds Slice and Dice unless boss/2+ enemies; shipped uses it 4–6 times, but no damage/truth efficacy measurement here.
