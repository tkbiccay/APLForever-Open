## Distinct problem and the released-source-only correction

[Previous pre-10 PR #62](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/pull/62) recorded that the published `BeastMasteryTurret` WoWSims fixture has **no Raptor Strike in its simulated kit**, even though its original released [`learn_hunter.lua`](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/tests/Forever/wowsims/learn_hunter.lua) learns **ID 2973 at level 1**. The actual released [Hunter.lua](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili_Hunter/Hunter.lua) declares its ranks, off-GCD on-next-swing queue, cooldown and spending, and released [hunter_world.lua](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/tests/Forever/helpers/hunter_world.lua) already models Raptor's known Mana costs, 0–5yd eligibility, queued next-swing strike and resource payment. No spell data was invented.

This entirely **test-only** experiment uses the *published* `Forever.helpers.wowsims.content` `kit = {"raptor_strike"}` source-augmentation interface to add this actual, already implemented source class ability to BM Turret for one source world. It also runs the original **unaugmented** BM Turret for a 4yd negative control. Class/source files, addon engine, all stored APLs, player settings, native Require Toggle flags, form/range and levelling spell IDs remain untouched. It is **not** a fully realistic level-1 Hunter character, pet, weapons or Mana.

Level 9 `strikeMana=150` reflects the published rank/cost table's rank 2 cost **25 Mana per strike**; earlier levels paid **10 Mana per strike**. An **accepted off-GCD Raptor queue is not itself an on-GCD filler**, and 7 queued/6 paid strikes do not constitute three successful on-GCD attacks. The seventh queue may remain until a future swing after the bounded 42s run. **This proves a positive source melee path with real payment and swings when the correct kit is exposed**, but it does not prove a playable early Hunter solo rotation in an actual client or any DPS/mitigation superiority.

**Source-world limitations:** It still retains BM Turret's anomalous **already tamed pet at level 1**, level-60 weapon stats, flat **3750 Mana** and incomplete early Auto Shot/live hunter positioning. No gear-accurate level-one Mana, pet-acquisition quest, true Hunter movement/facing or pet damage; the source's Auto Shot helper is not evidence of in-client automatic shooting/attack damage. A 6yd player must reposition manually; neither Raptor nor ranged shot is legal at 6yd, so do not invent a spell to fill that range. The 12yd level-one world still has **no modelled source-accepted offensive button** despite potential auto attacks.

**Regression and fallback:** Source-verified 4yd positive Raptor paid-strike outcome, source-verified no inappropriate off-range 6/12yd Raptor presses, exact unchanged 12yd Current/v1.4 behaviour and no repeated source-refused custom combat icons.
