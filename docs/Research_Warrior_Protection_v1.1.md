**Original observed issue:** Exact v1.0 wraps its self-contained `actions.survival` list, consisting exclusively of *resource-reserved Shield Block* variants, inside `call_action_list,name=survival,if=toggle.defensives&combat>0`. When optional Defensives OFF, source fails to recommend a routine, eligible Shield Block (not an actual user gameplay report). A v0.11 **in-memory** one-line alternative changes only that list call to `combat>0&aggro&(group|active_enemies>=2|target.is_boss|time>=10)`, retaining all original conditional Rage reserves and full final fallback.
