The revised test preserves raw error counts and permits only an isolated *positive* ≤`1e-7`-second `conflagrate`/`casting` boundary (≤1 per full-Mana seed); every genuinely substantive and every other refusal remains a failure. On the final green run, Current had **zero substantive refusals** in all tested scenarios. Shipped also had narrow casting boundaries: at 25%-Mana one Conflagrate on seed 30437 and at 5%-Mana one Life Tap on each seed; these are recorded, not hidden by an all-clean claim. Any different/new failure still requires investigation.
