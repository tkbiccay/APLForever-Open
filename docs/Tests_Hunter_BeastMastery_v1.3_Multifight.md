# Hunter BM v1.3 — multi-fight source-native test (20 November 2026)

**Question:** Does our new independently gated Bestial Wrath v1.3 behave safely when six-yard combat repeats three times on the **same addon instance**, compared to protected complete BM v1.2? Previous [v0.11.0 single-fight four-case run](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44808310285) proved one source-accepted Bestial Wrath at 6yd without dropping Hawk; the previously [merged BM three-fight run](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44807982020) only tested 12yd with Major OFF (shipped, v1.1, v1.2), not the new v1.3 at 6yd with Major ON.

**Pinned engine:** published [Hekili Forever v0.11.0](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/releases/tag/v0.11.0), commit `86b739cbc4e6afbcbc693c3790ee81197fc6234b`. Directly import each whole immutable protected v1.2 or candidate v1.3 APL, a separate original client for each, then re-use that loaded addon for three 35-second six-yard worlds (different seeds, same local ability kit, source Mana and native cooldown state). Read `run.pressed` rather than only recommendation icons; record per-fight Hawk, Bestial Wrath, accepted follow-ons, resource tail, native world events/refusals. Six-yard Rapid Fire/ranged attacks are forbidden. At least three real accepted actions must follow the first source-accepted Hawk each pull. No fabricated pet arrival, pet damage, real game client or DPS.

**Pre-declared failure criteria:** any engine errors, source refused casts, out-of-range ranged attacks, zero Hawks, less than three legal post-Hawk actions, fewer v1.3 Hawks or accepted actions than v1.2 in a matched pull, or missing the initial candidate six-yard Bestial Wrath that motivated v1.3. These fail the experiment, preserve the exact protected v1.2 and demand root-cause investigation; never silently loosen requirements.

**Status at authoring:** Native unrun; no effect on role selection, last-working reference or Approved. Real-game threat, pet activity, 1–60, solo group/boss DPS and safe cooldown consumption remain unverified.
