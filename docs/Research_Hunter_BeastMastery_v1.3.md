# Research — Hunter BM v1.3 pet cooldown independence (20 November 2026)

**Role:** Hunter Beast Mastery DPS, 1–60 planned, native source experiment at level 60 only. Equal top priority with Guardian Tank. Complete original v1.2 APL is exact and unselected, SHA256 `6abe7e0ff17ff3bfadf9dd8fe234399457127c3535272447b355120f2f5325cd`. v1.1 Current remains unchanged, `preferred_apl=null`, no last-working or Approved APL.

**Exact pin:** Hekili Forever [v0.11.0 Hunter.lua](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili_Hunter/Hunter.lua) (`86b739cbc4e6afbcbc693c3790ee81197fc6234b`) registers **Bestial Wrath** as off-GCD 120-second pet-dependent buff with 18-second window and native Cooldowns toggle, not a ranged shot with minimum eight-yard range. Arcane Shot has 8–35 yards and Summon Hawk 0–35 yards. Source-registered `window.hha` cannot force Arcane at six yards; HHA experiment [merged #24](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/pull/24) rejected pure HHA.

**Default comparison:** [shipped BM Turret translated APL](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili_Hunter/APLs/Beast%20Mastery%20Turret%20(wowsims.com).simc) uses Bestial Wrath at `time>=5` with no `target.outside8` requirement. Its repeated out-of-range ranged-shot recommendations at six yards are still unsafe; the rest of its rotation is a *reference*, not mandatory. WoWSims source pin `ab0f00e4fc83`, full independent default and v1.2 comparisons planned. Cross-check Wowhead, Icy Veins, Maxroll, Mobalytics and other credible Forever guides when a relevant build-matched description is verifiable; absent Forever coverage must be logged rather than use Retail.

**Hypothesis:** custom v1.2 places **all** Major cooldowns inside `call_action_list,if=toggle.cooldowns&combat>0&target.outside8`. This unintentionally withholds **Bestial Wrath** while the BM pet is alive and the Hunter is at the legal six-yard Hawk range. New full v1.3 hoists Bestial Wrath into one global off-GCD line with `toggle.cooldowns&combat>0&pet_out`, and removes the duplicate from major_cooldowns. Other Major cooldowns, Rapid Fire range safety, pet recovery, ranged/melee actions, HHA limitation and final fallback remain byte-for-byte unchanged.

**Test:** imported full v1.2 versus full candidate v1.3, actual source casts, 6/12 yards, Major OFF/ON, no 8-yard ranged shots at six yards, 3 post-Bestial accepted presses, 0 refusals, 0 errors, living pet, conservative source resource, stable repeated fallback. Exact negative/positive differences and overall validity determined by source-native CI; output gain, pet attack target, in-game behaviour not known. No new settings dependency.

**Initial status:** Draft, native and in-game tests pending. Protect v1.1/v1.2 and all SHA hashes. A safe source-native result supports merging as a candidate only; no selection or Approved until client observations.
