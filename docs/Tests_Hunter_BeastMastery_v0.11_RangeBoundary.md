# Hunter BM v0.11 — exact 8/35yd boundary and independent range-only architecture

**21 November 2026, Sprint 1000 launch checkpoint, source-native NEGATIVE design result.** [Published Hekili Forever v0.11.0](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/releases/tag/v0.11.0) pinned to `zmsl/hekili@86b739cbc4e6afbcbc693c3790ee81197fc6234b`. Full unselected [BM v1.4 Draft](../roles/Hunter/Candidates/APL_Hunter_BeastMastery_v1.4_Draft.txt), protected [Current v1.1 Draft](../roles/Hunter/Current/APL_Hunter_BeastMastery_v1.1_Draft.txt), shipped WoWSims BM comparator and an in-memory *full-APL* alternative; **NO new saved APL revision, no selection, no gameplay or DPS claim**.

**Why investigate:** [Source range semantics](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili/Engine/Sim/State.lua) use LibRangeCheck's bracket lower bound in `target.outside8` (`lower >= 8`), and individual known-rank `action.*.in_range` from `C_Spell.IsSpellInRange`, which only returns **false** when known unusable for range and may fail open on nil. The [published Hunter source-world SpellRange](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/tests/Forever/helpers/hunter_world.lua) is 8–35yd for ordinary shots/stings and 0–35yd for Summon Hawk. Hypothesis: v1.4's combined guard accidentally suppresses castable shots around *exactly 8 yards*. Alternative: remove only the four coarse `target.outside8&` ranged-shot clauses, leaving individual spell range guards, all other complete actions and pet/cooldown controls unchanged **in memory**. Do not mistake a syntactic simplification for improvement.

**[First exact-release source-native CI 44898520234](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44898520234): 7/7 PASS, zero failures/errors.** Seven static-distance worlds (6, 7.9, 8, 9, 10, 35, 35.1 yards) × four complete profiles = **28 matched 42-second source-world executions**, same seed 30417, actual accepted presses and source refusals, original level-60 BM kit, 3750 source Mana, living starting pet, native Major/Defensives/Movement OFF. In memory alternative required **exactly four** guards to be replaced, leaving the whole original APL otherwise unchanged. No injected resource, target-range claims, Hawk spawning, fake DPS or changes to saved full APLs.

**Source result:** The original suspected eight-yard false-negative does **not exist in these source worlds**. At 8.0yd the range library returns lower bound **8**, not 5, and `target.outside8` is already true. At 8/9/10yd the first actually accepted candidate casts are **Serpent Sting → Summon Hawk → Summon Hawk → Arcane Shot → Serpent Sting** (all legal, zero refusals). At 35.0yd source `C_Spell.IsSpellInRange` remains true and v1.4 still casts legally. Above 35yd, no ranged shot or Hawk is source-accepted; both custom profiles make only **two noncombat setup actions**, with no first-three combat proof—this is a legitimate out-of-range no-action endpoint, not three successful combat presses. At 6/7.9yd existing v1.4 produces five accepted Hawks and no refused 8–35yd ranged attacks; the extra bracket guard is not limiting Hawk.

**Decision:** **REJECT the in-memory range-only rewrite; do not create v1.5.** There is no source-accepted-action benefit over complete v1.4 in **any** tested distance state and removing a conservative guard can increase exposure to uncertain/unknown spell-range observations in the actual client (the test did **not** simulate a nil range). Preserve all **45** full historical APLs/hashes, BM v1.1 Current, unselected v1.4 and null BM preferred/last-working. The shipped default's repeated **209 rejected source presses** at 6 and 35.1 yards are a source-fixture range issue, **not** measured gameplay damage. Its extra 17 legal actions in the 8–35yd band do not establish a higher DPS objective. No revision or promotion warranted.

**Scope/next:** Only published source-native ranged checks, not real target movement, actual range brackets under latency/secrets, odd-sized hitboxes, live Hawk AI, pet recovery, boss threat or cross-class transfer.
