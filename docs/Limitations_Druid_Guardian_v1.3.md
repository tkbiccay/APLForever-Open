# Limitations — Druid Guardian v1.3 (19 November 2026)

- Real **group+tanking** positive path is unverified in the headless source world; game source documents those expressions as readable, but its fixture does not model a real player party threat relationship. No artificial positive proof; need a short safe open-world-group/dungeon `/hek snap` if the expression fails, or source-faithful fixture evidence.
- `target.within5` may read true while range is unbracketed. This pre-existing v1.2 guard is retained, not falsely certified as safe melee. Watch premature Barkskin/Roar or refused range casts.
- Hidden player health/incoming damage forbids emergency health triage; Barkskin remains a toggle-controlled proactive policy, not observed damage reduction. No numerical mitigation, TPS, DPS or multi-fight optimality was tested.
- Time >=5 and 10-Rage reserve are test hypotheses; do not assume optimal on every elite/dungeon/raid encounter. Mage/Hunter class mechanics cannot be transferred without role-specific proof.
- `settings.maintain_demoralizing_roar` is **OFF in player game defaults**, but the native `sim` scenario defaults ON for raid simulation. Explicitly force both in source tests.
- Positive natural high-Rage Maul remains **Blocked** in issue #3; v1.0 last-reported-working remains preferred. Full v0.10.0 role compatibility remains Partial due unrelated Feral/Arms warnings.
