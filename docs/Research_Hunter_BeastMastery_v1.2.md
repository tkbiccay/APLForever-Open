- **Default-first comparator:** published Beast Mastery Turret, then archived custom v1.0 and current Draft custom v1.1. The released source-native six-yard test first discovered a shipped **Serpent Sting out-of-range repeat**: 109 rejected `range` presses / 22 seconds in [run 44488313535](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44488313535). Both custom v1.0 and v1.1 avoided that bug with zero refusals, but v1.1 reached a safe **NO_ACTION** when neither normal 8–35yd ranged actions nor <=5yd melee actions were available.
- Independent hypothesis: allow **Summon Hawk only** within its own verified range and only with **`pet.hawk.count<2`**. Preserve strict 8yd checks on ranged shots; do not relax melee gates, pet recovery guards or cooldown switches.

## Selection and regression control

A real pre-existing pair of **silent-candidate-selection hazards** was identified before registering v1.2. `scripts/build_native_manifest.py` used the highest compatible revision by number when `preferred_apl=null`; `scripts/validate_repository.py` also interpreted `latest_revision` as Current. The release-native manifest now uses a compatible **Current**, falling back to **Archive** only for historical source compatibility (e.g. Hunter v1.0 on v0.8.2), never an unselected Candidate. Layout validation now follows the declared Current/Candidates paths; both have added regression tests. Explicit compatible preferred references remain honoured.

**No catalogue preference or working status changed.** `latest_revision=v1.2` records creation, not promotion. All **32 protected historical APL bytes/hashes remain untouched**; v1.2 adds the 33rd separately hashed revision. Rollback/development comparator is exact v1.1, **not** a claim that v1.1 is Approved or Reported Working.

## Risk and next acceptance

The published Hunter source models guardian count/expiry and source-world accepted casts, but cannot prove live Hawk spawning, pet DPS, actual Auto Shot suppression at six yards, full mana spend, facing, dynamic targets, pet recovery transitions or optimal timings. A new source-accepted Hawk can spend Mana in the dead zone without evidence of superior net damage; verify utility in-game. Level 1–60, solo, elite, dungeon and raid full coverage remains incomplete. Confirm gameplay `/hek snap` and several consecutive real first icons before changing lifecycle status. This is a **Draft Candidate only**, with no automated promotion. See [Tests](Tests_Hunter_BeastMastery_v1.2.md) and [PR #8](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/pull/8).
