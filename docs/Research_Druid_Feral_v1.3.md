**20 November 2026 — Draft, NOT SELECTED, source-native only.** Class Druid / Feral DPS; source Hekili Forever **v0.10.0**, commit `3c2cf3e32472cc807b1ba29d992fc13a273da8ee`. All other v1.2 lines, lists and the final fallback remain **identical**; this is not a diff-based import or a shared runtime dependency.

**Why:** v1.0 and v1.1 low-health defensive conditions depend on combat-hidden `health.pct` and cannot work; v1.2 removes those, but its only Barkskin path requires `tanking = role.tank & aggro`. The Forever `Feral` client's test role is DAMAGER. Source `aggro` uses `UnitThreatSituation("player")>1`, while observed group/AoE and melee range are limited but documented. The candidate's **single change** requires observed threat, party membership or visible 2+ enemies, within-melee range, the native Defensives toggle, a known and ready Barkskin, no existing Barkskin buff, and `gcd.remains<=0`. No health threshold, new per-spec setting, manual checkbox requirement, or falsely off-GCD spell.

**Negative architecture controls:** unconditional role-free aggro managed one accepted Barkskin but **one refused GCD action**; `line_cd=2` also failed to remove the refusal. Both are **REJECTED**. The published `gcd.remains` expression prevented that source-world refusal in matched conditions. This is not proof that a real client always knows threat/range, that mitigation is valuable, or that the ability can replace manual defensives.
