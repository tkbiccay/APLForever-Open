**Defect:** at L60 one attacker, 90 starting Rage, seed30417, 39.2 seconds after the source world's time zero, v1.2 attempted **Whirlwind at true Rage 24.94750** against **25 Rage** required; the published source refused `cost`. This is a **real cost refusal**, not a cast-end epsilon, not a refused action imported from another class. Normal v1.2 `rage.lo>=action.whirlwind.cost` exposed the Belief-vs-truth readiness uncertainty; the exact lower-bound error source is not fully identified. In the same matched world, v1.3 gave **0 refused actions**, its accepted 23 offensive/maintenance actions including nine Bloodthirst presses, and **0 Movement OFF violations**. The test asserts the v1.2 negative control stays present at the exact fixed seed and rejects any v1.3 raw refusal.

**Independent fallback and unlock checks:** all current and candidate runs made at least three true accepted presses, v1.3 had 17–20 accepted offensive actions in each L60 world and 18/19 accepted L24 offensives, with zero raw refusals in six worlds. Level24 v1.3 made **zero Bloodthirst presses** (not unlocked); level60 used it (8/9/9/10 by scenario/seed). Battle Shout or Berserker Stance post-upkeep was observed at L60; at L24 no *post-pull* upkeep press was captured, so **post-upkeep chronology is unproven there** even though 18/19 offensive casts were genuinely accepted. Final fallback rows were fully imported and reachable, but isolated branch coverage of each tail alternative is not implied by total action count.

**Movement OFF cross-check:** protected v1.0 issued **three accepted Intercepts in each L60 world** despite Movement OFF, exactly the unguarded old behaviour from historical tests; v1.2 and new v1.3 accepted **zero** Movement abilities while OFF in all six. The released fixture's *range model* does not independently prove the true client Charge/Intercept legal 8–25 yards, nor is Movement ON positively retested here. A 2026-11-17 v0.8.6 ON/OFF source test exists historically (see [v1.2 report](Tests_Warrior_Fury_v1.2.md)), but it cannot substitute for new release/live ON proof. Shipped used some movement actions while OFF; this is a comparison, not a user off-switch standard.

**Important caveat:** v1.3's safety margin reduced source-accepted actions versus v1.2 at L60 25 Rage/two attackers (21 vs 26 per seed). This could be legitimate avoidance of uncertain Rage or unnecessary holding; **no valid DPS/TTK/survival evidence establishes which**. Shipped accepted more actions but has materially different priorities/resource behaviour; raw counts are not comparative optimisation. The source world lacks real gear/player build, shield and threat efficacy, full 1–60 rank windows, movement/range ON, actual PvE 2+ target AoE damage, and live stale Rage/latency certainty. Unrun tests are clearly **UNVERIFIED**. Do not promote over protected Current v1.0 from headless evidence.

**Final fallback / repeated recommendation checkpoint:** v1.2 one refused Whirlwind at exact true cost recorded. New v1.3 had **zero repeated refused actions in this matrix**, zero raw refusals, active attacks after 3+ accepted presses and proper available talent spell boundary at L24/60. That does **not** prove the low-Rage player recommendation never waits excessively or that the conservative Whirlwind cushion is output-optimal. Next test: live `/hek snap` with actual Fury Rage around 25–35, Charge/Intercept valid range and Movement OFF/ON, 1/2 enemies and actual Rage source truth; headless follow-up longer 1–60 and alternative tighter +1/+2 margins before selecting. **Rollback is exact Current v1.0; all older files remain protected.**
