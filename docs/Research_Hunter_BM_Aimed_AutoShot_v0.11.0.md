# P0 Hunter BM Aimed Shot guard, source auto-shot clipping and Mana opportunity

**20 Nov 2026, research-only test.** Published Hekili Forever v0.11.0 exact `86b739cbc4e6afbcbc693c3790ee81197fc6234b`. Existing `v1.3` is full standalone unselected Draft; no APL, catalogue or protected rollback changes. Source [Hunter.lua](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili_Hunter/Hunter.lua) defines Aimed Shot as a hard 2s ranged cast (minus applicable haste), 6s shared cooldown with Multi-Shot, Mana cost; `auto_shot` is off-GCD `rangedSwing`, and `ranged_remains` a projected next auto time (0 when no reliable clock). Official [Beast Mastery Turret translated default](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili_Hunter/APLs/Beast%20Mastery%20Turret%20(wowsims.com).simc) suggests Aimed without our clipping guard. Our v1.3 guards Aimed with `ranged_remains>action.aimed_shot.cast+0.15`.

**Observed source discrepancy before experiment:** [three-fight native run 44807982020](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44807982020) at 12yd, 3 35-second fights on one boot, source-accepted shipped 10 Aimed, 12 Hawk, 5 Arcane, 40 actions and end Mana roughly 53; exact v1.1/v1.2 4 Aimed, 12 Hawk, 6 Arcane, 37 actions and end Mana roughly 1597. These counts are not DPS and v1.3 was not tested in that run. Alternative hypothesis: maybe our conservative anti-clip guard forfeits legitimate Aimed casts; counter-hypothesis: unguarded Aimed clips source-true ranged Auto Shot and burns Mana faster.

**Test** imports complete v1.3 and changes the **single** Aimed-shot priority line only in memory to drop its `ranged_remains` condition while retaining `target.outside8&action.aimed_shot.in_range`. Compare against source shipped and exact v1.3, three 35-second 12yd fights per profile, matched seeds 30418–30420, same 1.8s quiver source world and 30s out-of-combat gap; report real source `W.truth` ranged-hand swings (`kind=swing`, `hand=3`, `src=us`), matching `auto_shot` casts, Aimed/Arcane/Hawk/Serpent counts, Mana and accepted actions. Verify a 6yd relaxed variant cannot cast minimum-8yd ranged shots. No RNG output claim without three matched seed sets, never conflate accepted count with performance, and do not claim game DPS, real shoot timer or pet damage. If world doesn't expose one source counter, fail the experimental validity and classify test/harness limitation rather than fudge.

**Actual first negative result — fixture boundary:** [run 44809596783](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44809596783) failed the experiment's initial requirement for `W.truth` ranged `swing` events: the source actually recorded **20 real Auto Shot `kind=cast` events per first 35s fight** for all three tested profiles, but zero `kind=swing,hand=3` rows. Published [world.lua `W.startRanged`](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/tests/Game/harness/world.lua) produces a `kind=swing` only when caller provides optional `o.swing`; the published BM Turret world does not provide it. This is **harness/event-visibility fault**, not proof the hunter failed to auto-attack or that the APL is correct. Revised experiment requires source-true `auto_shot` casts and labels genuine swing events and clipping as **unmodelled**, never fake source swing counts. The aggressive Aimed one-line variant was also observed at 12yd with **4** Aimed vs v1.3's **0** in the first fight; true auto-cast count **20** for each. Without faithful cast-delay and damage modelling, this cannot justify promoting unguarded Aimed, even if source casts are accepted. New workflow completion is pending.

**Status at creation:** Unrun. No production APL changes. Next decision only after actual native results plus source validation whether auto-shot count difference is mechanically meaningful.
