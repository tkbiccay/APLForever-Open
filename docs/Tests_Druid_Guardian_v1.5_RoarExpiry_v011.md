## New hypothesis, distinct from PR #58

[PR #58's](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/pull/58) five-case 38-second role/aggro test established the *first* cast of v1.5's self-contained Demoralising Roar under client group threat even when the source client's pseudo-role was DAMAGER. It **did not prove a 30-second applied debuff expires, that subsequent upkeep recasts successfully, or that an accepted but resisted Roar is treated correctly**. The current test exercises four longer whole-file source-world states: two matched seed `30417/30418` **110s group/true-threat 4yd**, one `110s group/no-threat 4yd`, one `58s group/true-threat 6yd`. Each imports/runs complete shipped, working, and candidate = **12 full-profile world executions**. The source fake client role= DAMAGER, UnitThreatSituation and grouped membership are deliberately controlled inputs. **Rage, target truth, enemy swings, spell outcomes, actual debuff aura applications/expiry, and casts are from the published world**, not fabricated. Native Major/Defensives/Movement OFF. Each 4yd positive test supplies natural 88+ mob swings and Rage lower-bound peaks of 44.75; no artificial initial Rage.

[**Final strict source-native 44941415657**](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44941415657) **4/4 PASS, zero failures/errors**. Evidence explicitly distinguishes **accepted press** from actual successful debuff application by inspecting the published world's `truth` aura events for the real Roar ID `99` and observing natural aura expiration events. The addon source [druid_loop_world.lua](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/tests/Forever/helpers/druid_loop_world.lua) explicitly uses `OUTCOMES.demoralizing_roar = "spell"` and applies the debuff **only if the spell outcome is `WOUND`** (landed). Therefore even an accepted GCD/cost can legitimately fail to apply the debuff.

**Seed30418 resisted-cast sequence is important:** the four source-accepted Roars were at **17.6, 43.4, 77.0, 78.6 seconds**. **The 17.6s and 77.0s casts did NOT apply the debuff**. The latter is followed by a proper 1.6s retry when Rage/eligibility permit; the two real applied auras remained 35.2s apart. This is neither a duplicate active-aura Roar nor proof of DPS benefit. In seed30417 both accepted Roars actually landed at 9.6 and 53.0. Both seeds independently showed the debuff fall off; 6yd and no-threat negatives made zero accepted Roars and zero actual Roar auras. Native v1.5 accepted **zero refused source actions** and subsequent first-Roar three actual accepted actions; its first post-upkeep actions in seed30417 were Primal Bite → Lacerate → Primal Bite.

**Diagnostic correction without changing an APL:** initial [red 44940809269](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44940809269) and [timestamp red 44940992534](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44940992534) wrongly required **every accepted Roar press** to be 29+ seconds apart, assuming all presses landed. The 25.8s interval in seed30418 failed; source-truth probe [44941205038](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44941205038) isolated **two successful aura applications out of four accepted Roars**. Final strict test explicitly asserts **landed aura application intervals ≥29s** and if two accepted casts fall within 29s, the earlier cast must lack a matching actual landed `aura` truth event. This is a **corrected mechanical assertion, not a relaxed waiver of early duplicate applications**. Preserve negative runs and their root cause for other DoT/debuff tests.
