**95s low-Mana endpoint / Final Fallback / Repeated Recommendation:** world initial source Mana set to 55% of its stock 6000 and spent naturally thereafter; no arbitrary low-Mana value is injected mid-fight. Each of shipped/v1.0/v1.1 hit **≤25% actual Mana** at 8.2s and supplied three separate **actual enemy swing events**, not three unchanged displayed icons. Shipped accepted **1 Shield / 26 total** and finished with **3.8 true Mana**; v1.0 **0 Shield / 25 total**, **132.8 true Mana**; v1.1 **2 Shields / 27 total**, **4.0 true Mana**, all **0 refusals**. From low-Mana first reached, shipped had 19 accepted casts, Current 19, candidate 21. At three moving-world checkpoints, v1.1 recorded Consecration accepted at 8.2s then two Holy Strike recommendations with **positive waits** (1.30s / 1.10s), so **not** three ready-but-unaccepted icons. Current had an Exorcism accepted and two different wait-positive actions. Shipped recommendations were all wait-positive or varied; zero world refusals throughout. Test fails any three identical immediately-ready **known-wait** recommendations across source swing events with no accepted action; unknown waits are never coerced into zero. A `false` press from the source harness must be treated as **not accepted**. This checkpoint is limited to the three captured swing events, not a general proof of no stall at every level/resource/toggle.

**Caveats and decision:** Candidate v1.1 has **more source-accepted Shield casts** than shipped (4 vs 3 in matched 38s), and drains true Mana near zero by the 38s or 95s endpoints. That is **not evidence of better tank survival, threat, sustained output or efficiency**, and could crowd out damaging spells; combat damage/mitigation and player real resource belief remain unproved. The source Paladin world states its **damage, gear and Mana pool are unmeasured**, and its threat/role is artificially controlled for this test. The entire post-upkeep final priority remains protected; false threat and short solo avoid routine Shield. The test does not establish the actual in-game `Require Toggle` setting, exact levels 1–60, movement, multi-target target selection, or real-group aggro.

**Outcome:** v1.1 is **Source-Native Supported** for this narrow toggle/threat/Mana/fallback behaviour; still **unselected Draft / Pending In-Game Validation**, not Approved or a measured v1.0 upgrade.
