# Feral DPS stationary Travel-Form combat recovery — 20 Nov 2026

**Decision:** full independent v1.4 **unselected Draft**, not Approved. Hypothesis: v1.3 intentionally protects Travel/Aquatic/Moonkin by excluding them from all combat shape and caster branches, but when genuinely engaged and stationary in Travel Form this can create an actionable zero-recommendation tail. Automatically ending escape movement could itself regress gameplay; guard with **actual player movement**.

**Exact released source:** [Hekili Forever v0.11.0](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/tree/86b739cbc4e6afbcbc693c3790ee81197fc6234b), [Druid Cat Form](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/src/Hekili_Druid/Druid.lua), [Forever conditions](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.11.0/docs/wiki/APL-Conditions-On-Forever.md) documents `moving` as **Read**; released State.lua samples `GetUnitSpeed("player") > 0`. The class `cat_form` handler removes each other Druid form; target range and combat are subject to Forever engine observation/forecast. WoWSims-derived shipped Feral is the independent benchmark, not architectural policy. Forever-relevant outside guides reviewed in the Feral v1.1 role research; see [Method Forever Feral rotation](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/feral-dps-druid/rotation-and-playstyle) and [Icy Veins Forever Feral](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/feral-druid-melee-dps-and-tank-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities). Neither guide proves the addon should automatically leave an escape form.

**Full v1.3 source-native failure observation:** [PR #52 first v0.11 run 44820389529](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44820389529), 3/3 test cases. Published-source fake world starts at four yards in Travel Form with mob attacks and legitimate client form/combat events; **v1.3 accepted zero recommendations / zero Cat shifts / zero attacks across 38s despite 191 source combat ticks**. Shipped accepted 22 presses (one Cat, 16 attacks); test-only source-faithful in-memory change accepted 16 presses (one Cat, 14 attacks) and zero refusals. These are actions, not DPS or proven in-game behaviour.

**Escape intent negative and candidate:** [PR #52 movement run 44820645910](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44820645910), 4/4. A source-client GetUnitSpeed return of 7 before addon initialisation produced 101 client-moving ticks; both protected v1.3 and guarded in-memory change had **zero Cat shifts** and no refusals while travelling. Stationary normal Cat source controls matched **12 presses, 11 attacks**, no refusals. Player movement is readable in source, but client animation, latency, live actual movement and player intent remain outside the headless proof.

**One-line APL change:** For the main `actions+=/cat_form` line only, retain no auto-switch out of Bear/Aquatic/Moonkin. Permit exiting Travel when `combat>0&target.within5&!moving`; maintain the normal form shift when not in those forms. Precombat and every other full list/fallback retain v1.3 bytes. **No settings checkbox**, native Defensives/Major off-switches unchanged. Check [v1.4 exact code](../roles/Druid/Candidates/APL_Druid_Feral_v1.4_Draft.txt) and [v1.3 comparator](../roles/Druid/Candidates/APL_Druid_Feral_v1.3_Draft.txt).

**Stored-file proof:** [exact v1.4 native 4/4 PASS, run 44821443310](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44821443310), full stored APL byte-equal to in-memory hypothesis and matched three variants; repository [validator PASS, run 44821443354](https://github.com/tkbiccay/APLForever-Open/actions/runs/44821443354). **Acceptance boundary:** human client must validate standing in Travel while attacked, deliberately escaping while moving, first Cat cast plus three ordinary casts and source off-switches. If in-game movement prediction flickers or user escape is disrupted, retain v1.3/Current rather than promote. No measured damage, health, survival, all-level or hostile aggro proof.
