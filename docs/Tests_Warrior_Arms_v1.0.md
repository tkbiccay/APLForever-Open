## Explicit limitations, policy and next evidence

- **Low-level source world inherits a level-60 two-handed weapon and unlevelled damage constants.** Natural Rage income, enemy damage, throughput and level-specific rotation effectiveness are not realistic levelling results; no seed-set DPS gain asserted.
- **One/two incoming attackers is not a verified offensive `active_enemies=2` mode**. Neither source-world incoming counts nor the printed zero stance transitions proves that native Hekili saw two valid AoE targets, or that `cleave_and_aoe`, Whirlwind and manual AoE targeting were reached. A dedicated source-observable AoE/stance test remains **Not Run**.
- **Bloodrage health-cost risk (HEALTH-013):** The shipped pack used the registered Bloodrage action even with this custom-style Minor Cooldowns toggle OFF; the Current Arms APL contains a *conditional* precombat Bloodrage under `toggle.minor_cooldowns` and `health.pct>=95` on bosses. This source world models Rage gain but **does not model actual health loss**, and live Forever HP visibility may be secret. This test deliberately disabled minor cooldowns and did not simulate a boss. **Do not promote, remove or loosen the health guard on simulation counts alone**; actual autonomous health-cost safety remains a separate engine/gameplay gate.
- **Unverified:** all rank-family variants beyond first known trainer unlock; exact Rage cost boundaries and belief mismatch; proc/Overpower reaction timing versus visible observer; positive-wait starvation, execute, real AoE/manual targeting and target swaps; Major/Minor/Defensive toggles ON; real cooldowns, range/facing/movement, combat latency, live threat/DPS and gameplay reliability. This is bounded source-native coverage, **not 1–60 comprehensive testing**.
- The full cross-guide assessment (Wowhead, Icy Veins, Maxroll, Mobalytics, WoWSims and current Forever-only theorycrafting) is **not claimed** from this test-only sprint; require it before designing a new substantial Arms revision. Do not import non-Forever rotations.
- **Next efficient gate:** first verify the source can observe *offensive active-enemy count* and a real Berserker Stance/Whirlwind-eligible two-target window (not merely two hostile attackers), then run a genuinely state-advanced AoE/stance/final-tail comparison. Alternatively, if source cannot represent this accurately, mark **Engine Limitation** and request one small normal-game `/hek snap` only when it presents naturally. Do not churn a new v1.1 candidate solely because the shipped pack accepted more actions in an unpriced health/throughput world.

**Decision:** **Retain exact standalone Current v1.0, catalogue `StaticValidated`, all 29 hashes, and no working/approved selection.** No new APL promoted and no claims of superior throughput. Treat the shipped pack as a benchmark; a future independent candidate must beat it *and* this archival Current on faithfully matched castability, practical output and safety evidence.

**Grounded mechanics, NOT guessed AoE from incoming attackers:** [Official v0.8.6 target-counting guide](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.8.6/docs/wiki/How-Targets-Are-Counted.md), [source target counter](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/blob/v0.8.6/src/Hekili/Engine/Sim/Targets.lua) and normal [native observer/scenarios](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili/tree/v0.8.6/tests/Game/harness) support separate source-emitted hostile nameplates. No test directly sets `state.active_enemies`, creates fictitious targets, grants Rage or injects APL conditions.

**Interpretation:** The complete custom APL correctly receives the two-target condition and successfully executes its Berserker Stance → Whirlwind → Battle Stance cycle at **level 40**. At **level 60** it issued **no accepted Berserker Stance and no Whirlwind casts** in the matched real two-target condition under this seed, even though the source counted two targets throughout. This is a genuine **bounded AoE activation gap relative to the shipped comparator**, NOT evidence of a refused cast, three-swing fallback error, broken engine count, actual DPS disadvantage or a new regression caused by release v0.8.6. The APL's `stance_rage_kept`/Rage-floor window and its Mortal Strike cooldown, Rend remains and Overpower conditions are **plausible gating contributors, not yet root-cause proved**; do not simplify/remove guards on conjecture.

**Critical output and environment limits:** The released `Forever.helpers.warrior_world` `M.MAP.whirlwind` effect does only `W.hit(e, e.target, ...)`: **it does not simulate hits on a second target**. Therefore these worlds prove *counting, castability, Rage opportunity windows and stance control*, NOT comparative two-target damage or DPS. Shipped also uses Bloodrage, which is modelled as Rage gain **without its real health cost**; apparent extra casts cannot establish a safe higher-output rotation. Lower-level gear/damage, true live client enemy plates at reload/in instances, range/facing and manual target swaps remain limitations. APL alone cannot switch on the game/client's **Show Enemy Nameplates / Show All Nameplates** CVars: nameplate visibility and spec's **Count Nameplates Near You** are a **one-time required engine/UI setting**, not additional manual combat actions. Verified transfer method [AOE-015](knowledge/lessons/CROSS_APL_TRANSFER.md) applies development-time across specs, not runtime APL code.

**Decision:** Keep exact Current v1.0 `3bb923e9b071a3d9432471731ce609a3e8852741364ce4334d136e4509335f2c` and all 29 immutable catalogue hashes. Still **StaticValidated**, no preferred/last-working/Approved selection, no Candidate promoted. **Next one *distinct* headless check**, only if advancing Arms before Fury: at level 60 with two genuinely source-counted enemies and normal source Rage, trace the exact times each part of the stance/Whirlwind guard becomes true or false (Rage `lo`, `stance_rage_kept`, Mortal Strike CD, Rend remaining, Overpower proc), then test a *standalone* Candidate against shipped and Current on fair repeatable real-outcome evidence. The existing source world cannot prove two-target damage, so do **not** create a superior-DPS claim or run another redundant 16-case sweep. If a faithful AoE damage oracle is absent, mark Engine Limitation and proceed to Warrior Fury rather than speculative priority churn.
