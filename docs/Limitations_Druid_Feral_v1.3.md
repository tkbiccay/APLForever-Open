# Limitations — Druid Feral DPS v1.3

**Source-native only, not Approved.** v0.10.0 `aggro` may be hidden/unknown in dungeons; `active_enemies` may read 1 without nameplates and in instances; `group` tracks members rather than player-target threat. Our test controls the client threat API *before source load* and adds learned Barkskin and its registered buff to the development world because the stock Feral source world lacks it. This proves only one accepted Barkskin + aura with zero refused actions in one matched simulated group state, not future danger, mitigation effectiveness, survival, or DPS.

Low-health reactive Frenzied Regeneration and Nature's Grasp are **deliberately manual**, because actual player health is hidden in combat; do not use fabricated HP/TTL or hidden combat log. Cannot guarantee the `aggro` signal in real multiplayer content. The `gcd.remains` guard is source-supported and passed scoped test, not proof for all levels/gear/talents/GCD conditions. Defensive ON/OFF and repeated fallback must be checked live. v1.0 Current and v1.2 prior Candidate are protected by their SHA-256; v1.0 is a **Draft**, not a known good working version. No default/last-working output benchmark and no player validation are yet complete.
